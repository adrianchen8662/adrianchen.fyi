For this week's discussion topic, we were to find an article on dbta.com and write a summary. 

This is the article I've decided to use: https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/Trends-and-Applications/RESEARCH-at-DBTA-Data-and-AI-Survey-All-Systems-Go-But-is-Everything-Ready-177144.aspx

This article summarizes the findings of a 2026 survey from Drexel University's LeBow College of Business. The main finding is that AI is important to 52% of organizations' data strategies. Conversely, 43% of respondents feel data issues are significant barriers to success with AI. The article highlights the contradiction in the companies feeling confident about AI readiness. Companies cite data and technology infrastructure as a major challenge.
